About Us

Discover the vision behind Voices of the Diaspora

What is Voices of the Diaspora?

Voices of the Diaspora is a digital platform that brings together news, podcasts, videos, and social media about the global African Diaspora. Inspired by COMM 300 at Morgan State University, this project engages students and provides a central resource to explore Black identity in media. It highlights diverse voices, stories, and cultures through content like music, film, and language exchange. By fostering learning and cultural connection, this platform helps unify and amplify African Diaspora narratives worldwide.

What is COMM 300?

COMM 300 (Communication and the Black Diaspora) is a course offered through the School of Global Journalism and Communication at Morgan State University. Open to all undergraduate students, it fulfills the critical thinking general education requirement.

The course helps students understand the African Diaspora, focusing on the modern era shaped by the Transatlantic Slave Trade and European colonization. Topics include shared cultural elements—language, music, film, dance, food, and spirituality—as well as how media portrays these communities and how they use media to tell their own stories.

Who is the intended audience?

This platform is designed for students and educators, providing resources to learn about and teach the history, culture, and global impact of the African Diaspora. Whether for classroom use or independent exploration, this platform offers tools to connect with the richness of African-descended communities worldwide.
