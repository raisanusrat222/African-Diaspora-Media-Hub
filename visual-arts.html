Traditional Kente Weaving

Ghana, West Africa

Traditional hand-woven cloth made from silk and cotton threads. Each pattern has specific symbolic meaning representing proverbs, historical events, or important figures in Akan culture.

Cultural Evolution

African American Quilting

United States, North America

Adapted textile tradition using available materials like cotton scraps. Maintained symbolic storytelling through geometric patterns, often encoding messages and preserving cultural memory.
